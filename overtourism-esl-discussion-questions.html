Overtourism ESL Discussion Questions

15 conversation questions about crowded destinations, tourism, local residents and who gets to shape a city.
A1–A2 BeginnerB1–B2 IntermediateC1–C2 Advanced

Use these overtourism ESL discussion questions for pair work, small-group speaking or whole-class conversation. Choose the level that fits your learners, or move through all three sections as the ideas become more challenging.

A

Beginner · A1–A2

Start the conversation

  1. 01

    Have you visited a place with too many people? How did you feel?

  2. 02

    Do many tourists come to your town or city? What do they come to see?

  3. 03

    Why can tourists and residents feel differently about the same place?

  4. 04

    Is it fair to ask tourists to pay a fee? Why or why not?

  5. 05

    Who does a famous city belong to: the residents or the visitors?

B

Intermediate · B1–B2

Explore the issue

  1. 01

    Have you ever visited a place that felt too crowded? What was it like?

  2. 02

    Has tourism changed a place you know well? How?

  3. 03

    Why might residents and tourists see the same city so differently?

  4. 04

    Do you think entry fees or visitor limits are fair? Why or why not?

  5. 05

    Who do you think a famous city really belongs to—its residents or its visitors?

C

Advanced · C1–C2

Challenge the idea

  1. 01

    Would you rather see a famous place packed with crowds, or not see it at all? Why?

  2. 02

    Has a place you know been changed by outsiders? Who gained, who lost, and who decided?

  3. 03

    Why do tourism’s profits and its costs so often land on different people?

  4. 04

    Is Venice’s entry fee a sensible first step, or merely a symbolic gesture?

  5. 05

    Should residents have the final say over their city’s future, even if visitors pay for it?
