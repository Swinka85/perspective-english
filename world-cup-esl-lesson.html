ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This World Cup ESL Lesson

Every four years, the World Cup turns football into a truly global event. Billions of people follow the tournament, entire countries celebrate or suffer together, and moments on the pitch can become part of national history.

This free B1–B2 World Cup ESL lesson explores why an international football tournament can mean so much to people around the world. Students consider the history and global influence of the World Cup, football culture, national identity and why major sporting events can unite people who may have little else in common.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss the World Cup, international football, global sporting events, fans, national identity and whether the tournament has become something much larger than sport.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Every four years, something remarkable happens. For one month, people who have never kicked a ball gather around screens in homes, cafés, and public squares, united by a single tournament. Strangers hug after a goal; entire nations hold their breath together. The World Cup is football, but it is also something much larger.
