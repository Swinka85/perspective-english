ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Experiences vs Possessions ESL Lesson

Experiences are increasingly sold as an alternative to material possessions, but status, comparison and consumerism have followed us into the experience economy.

This C1–C2 Experiences vs Possessions ESL lesson examines why experiences may bring lasting satisfaction, how businesses capitalize on that shift, and whether posting experiences online simply creates a new form of materialism.

Students work with advanced expressions, test their spending choices under a social-media twist, defend an authentic tourism product under challenge and write an extended argument addressing the strongest opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Outside a small bakery, the line forms before opening and curls around the corner. Few of those waiting will eat what they buy while it is still warm. They will hold it up to the light, photograph it and post it before the first bite. The bread is excellent. But the line has very little to do with bread.
