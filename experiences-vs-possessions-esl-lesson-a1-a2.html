ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Experiences vs Possessions ESL Lesson

People spend money on both things and experiences, but many younger consumers now say they prefer trips, concerts and other moments to possessions.

This A1–A2 Experiences vs Possessions ESL lesson helps beginner learners talk about spending, happiness, memories, social media, showing off and what makes something worth buying.

Students use a short original reading and audio, beginner vocabulary, true-or-false comprehension, five discussion questions, a same-price decision activity, a tourism-company speaking challenge and guided writing.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Outside a small bakery, the line starts before it opens and goes around the corner. Most people will not eat their bread while it is warm. First, they photograph it and post it online. The bread is good. But the line is not really about bread.
