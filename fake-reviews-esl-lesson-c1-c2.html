ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Fake Reviews ESL Lesson

Online reviews were meant to be a digital form of word of mouth, but paid praise, fabricated complaints and AI-generated text have made credibility increasingly difficult to judge.

This C1–C2 Fake Reviews ESL lesson examines how businesses and reviewers can game the system, why consumers take ratings at face value, and whether regulation, platforms or skeptical readers should carry the greatest responsibility.

The lesson develops advanced discussion and argumentation through idiomatic vocabulary, evidence-based comprehension, a six-review Spot the Fake challenge, a defended café proposal and an extended opinion-writing task.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Online reviews were meant to be a digital form of word of mouth: honest advice passed between customers. Instead, they have become an industry. Businesses can buy hundreds of five-star ratings for roughly the price of a meal.
