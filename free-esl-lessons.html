COMMON QUESTIONS

Free ESL lessons: the essentials

Are these ESL lessons really free?

Yes. The five topics on this page are free at all three levels. Open a lesson page to access its student worksheet, teacher guide, audio and presentation.

Which English levels are available?

Each topic has an A1–A2 beginner version, a B1–B2 intermediate version and a C1–C2 advanced version. Choose the version that best fits your students.

Can I use these lessons in adult or university classes?

Yes. The materials are designed for discussion-focused English teaching, including adult learners and university classes. Teachers can adapt the pace and activities to their students.

How long does each lesson take?

Teaching time is flexible. It depends on your learners, class size, how deeply you explore the discussion questions and which extension activities you choose.
