ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Advanced Space Tourism ESL Lesson

Space tourism has turned an experience once limited to professional astronauts into something private customers can buy. For now, however, a few minutes beyond Earth remain available only to the extremely wealthy.

This C1–C2 lesson explores the arguments surrounding private spaceflight: technological progress, extreme cost, environmental impact and whether access to space should depend on wealth.

Students weigh competing claims to a scarce seat in space before developing and defending a condition for public support of private tourist flights.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Supporters argue that every great technology begins as a toy for the rich. Air travel and mobile phones were once luxuries too, and early customers helped pay for the research that made them cheaper.
