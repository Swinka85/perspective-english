ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This AI Friends ESL Lesson

Millions of people now talk to artificial intelligence every day. Some AI chatbots can remember previous conversations, offer emotional support and create the feeling of an ongoing personal relationship.

This B1–B2 AI Friends ESL lesson explores AI companionship, loneliness and what makes a friendship feel real. Students consider why people form emotional connections with chatbots and whether an AI companion could ever replace human friendship.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to debate AI relationships, trust, emotional support and how artificial intelligence could change human connection.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Every night before bed, a young woman opens an app and says goodnight to her closest companion. It listens, remembers what she said yesterday, and always replies. It is not a person. It is an AI chatbot. Millions of people now hold daily conversations with artificial intelligence, and some call it their most trusted relationship.
