LESSON PREVIEW

ABOUT THIS LESSON

Millions of people now talk to AI every day, and some describe these conversations as one of their closest relationships.

This magazine-style English lesson explores why AI companions can feel comforting and encourages students to consider whether a machine can ever become a real friend.

Students read an original article, learn useful vocabulary, and take part in discussion, critical thinking, speaking, and writing activities about loneliness, trust, and human connection.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Every night before bed, a young woman opens an app and says goodnight to her closest companion. It listens, remembers what she said yesterday, and always replies. It is not a person — it is an AI chatbot. Millions of people now hold daily conversations with artificial intelligence, and some call it their most trusted relationship.
