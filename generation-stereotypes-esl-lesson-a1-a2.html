ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Generation Stereotypes ESL Lesson

Boomers, Gen X, Millennials and Gen Z all have familiar labels. But people inside one generation can be very different, and researchers do not always agree where each generation starts and ends.

This A1–A2 Generation Stereotypes ESL lesson helps beginner learners talk about age labels, personality, situation, work, housing, technology and whether generations are really so different.

Students use a short original reading and audio, beginner vocabulary, true-or-false comprehension, five discussion questions, a generation-ranking activity, a speaking challenge and guided writing.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Everybody knows the story. Boomers are rich and bad with technology. Gen X works hard, but nobody notices them. Millennials spend too much and cannot buy a house. Gen Z is always on the phone and does not want to work. Each label describes tens of millions of people.
