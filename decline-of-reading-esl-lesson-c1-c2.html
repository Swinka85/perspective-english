ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Advanced The Decline of Reading ESL Lesson

People may be reading more words than ever, but much of that reading is fragmented across messages, headlines and short posts. Long-form reading now competes with a digital environment designed to divide attention.

This C1–C2 lesson explores whether deep reading is genuinely declining, what sustained attention gives us and whether concern about changing reading habits reflects evidence or nostalgia.

Students evaluate different forms of reading before proposing and defending a policy or practice designed to protect sustained reading without rejecting useful digital habits.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Reading has not disappeared, but the way we read is changing. Skimming headlines and short posts can keep us informed, while sustained reading asks us to slow down, follow an argument and resist distraction.
