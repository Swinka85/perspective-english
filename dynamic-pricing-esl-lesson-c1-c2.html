ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Dynamic Pricing ESL Lesson

Two passengers take the same flight but pay dramatically different fares. This advanced C1–C2 lesson examines why prices fluctuate and asks when market logic crosses the line into unfair treatment.

Students read and listen to an article about airline and hotel pricing, ride-hailing surges, algorithms, the going rate and the risk of turning customers into a captive audience. They consider transparency, supply and demand, and personalized pricing.

Groups then run a two-day music festival in the You Set the Price challenge, defend five pricing decisions during a radio-program role-play, and write a 200–250 word opinion addressing the strongest opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

If a price depends on who is buying, when they buy and how badly they want it, what is something actually worth?
