ENVIRONMENT & SUSTAINABILITY

Environmental & Sustainability ESL Lessons

Explore ready-to-teach B1–B2 ESL lessons about sustainability, consumption, pollution, climate, tourism and environmental responsibility. Each lesson turns a real-world issue into reading, vocabulary, discussion, critical thinking and meaningful classroom speaking.

Browse environmental ESL lessons → Explore all ESL news lessons

Environmental issues are ideal for ESL discussion because they connect everyday choices with bigger global questions. Students can examine what we buy, what we throw away, how we travel, what companies claim, and how societies respond to environmental pressure.

Perspective English environmental and sustainability ESL lessons are designed primarily for B1–B2 adult and university learners. The lessons combine original articles with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion, critical thinking, speaking activities, audio and teacher support.

Use the collection below to build a sustainability unit, connect several lessons around one theme, or choose a single issue that fits your class. The topics are designed to stay discussion-worthy beyond one news cycle.

B1–B2 Tourism Free

Overtourism

Explore what happens when popular destinations become overwhelmed by visitors, and debate how tourism can support local economies without damaging communities and places.
