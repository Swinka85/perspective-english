LESSON PREVIEW

ABOUT THIS LESSON

Heatwaves are becoming harder to ignore. In many cities, summer temperatures now regularly reach dangerous levels, affecting health, work, transport, energy use, and everyday life.

This magazine-style English lesson explores why extreme heat is becoming more common, how climate change can intensify heatwaves, and the difficult balance between preventing further warming and adapting to temperatures that are already rising.

Students read an original article and take part in vocabulary, discussion, critical-thinking, speaking, and writing activities about extreme heat, air conditioning, city planning, climate responsibility, adaptation, and who should pay for protection from a hotter world.

FROM THE ARTICLE

The New Normal? began as an advertisement. More than a century later, anonymous inspectors can change a restaurant's future with a single star. But when a simple bowl of noodles can be unforgettable and an expensive meal can disappoint, can anyone really decide what the world's best food is?
