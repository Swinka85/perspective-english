ABOUT THIS LESSON

Scientists have long searched for ways to help people live longer, healthier lives. Today, new discoveries are changing how we think about ageing.

This magazine-style English lesson introduces the latest research into healthy ageing and encourages students to explore how science could shape our future.

Students read an original article, learn useful vocabulary and discuss whether slowing the ageing process could become a reality.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Imagine reaching the age of 100 but still feeling forty. For most of history this was a fairy tale, yet in laboratories around the world scientists are now studying aging itself — not as something inevitable, but as a process that might one day be slowed.
