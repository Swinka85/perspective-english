ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Advanced Happiness and Money ESL Lesson

A long-awaited purchase can feel extraordinary at first, yet the thrill often fades surprisingly quickly. Psychologists describe this pattern as the hedonic treadmill: new pleasures become familiar, and people begin looking for the next source of satisfaction.

This C1–C2 lesson examines the relationship between wealth and contentment, including social comparison, status, financial security, experiences and the sacrifices people make while pursuing more money.

Students evaluate six financial and lifestyle choices for their long-term value, use advanced expressions such as “keep up with the Joneses” and “strike a balance,” and defend a workplace happiness proposal under challenge.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A man finally buys the car he has wanted for years. For a few weeks, every drive feels like a small celebration. Then, almost without his noticing, the novelty wears off, and the car becomes just another way to get to work.
