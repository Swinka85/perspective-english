ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Photography ESL Lesson

Do our photos help us remember, or do they stop us from enjoying the moment? In this beginner-friendly lesson, students read about concerts, restaurants and a museum study that found people with cameras remembered less.

Students learn five useful words, check what the article says, and talk about the photos on their own phones, food photos and phone bans at concerts.

Activities include choosing only three moments to photograph on a special day, a family phone-rule role-play and a short opinion-writing task.

What’s Included in Picture Perfect?

  • Magazine-style A1–A2 student worksheet (two pages)
  • Original reading and matching MP3 audio about taking photos
  • Five vocabulary items and five true-or-false reading comprehension questions
