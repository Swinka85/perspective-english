ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Overtourism ESL Lesson

Tourism can bring jobs, money and opportunities to cities around the world. But when millions of visitors arrive in the same destinations, tourism can also create crowded streets, rising prices, pressure on housing and frustration for local residents.

This free A1–A2 Overtourism ESL lesson introduces learners to the idea that too many visitors can create problems for popular places. Students explore how tourism affects local people and use accessible English to talk about busy destinations, travel and possible solutions.

The lesson combines an original level-appropriate reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion and speaking activities. Students use practical English to talk about tourism, travel, crowded places, local communities and responsible tourism.
