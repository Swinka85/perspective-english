ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This K-pop ESL Lesson

Is becoming a K-pop star worth the pressure? This intermediate ESL lesson looks at the K-pop trainee system: years of training from a young age, strict rules, intense competition and no guarantee of success.

Students weigh both sides, from the sacrifices young trainees make to the discipline, skills and rewards the system can bring, without discussing any real idol’s private life.

Activities include judging six parts of the trainee system as fair or unfair, a parent-and-teenager role-play about joining a training company, and an opinion-writing task.
