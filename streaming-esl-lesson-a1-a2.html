ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Streaming & Cinema ESL Lesson

Streaming makes it easy to watch movies at home, but cinemas offer a different experience. Students consider ticket prices, attention, and why people say they love cinemas even when they rarely visit.

This A1–A2 lesson combines an accessible original reading with audio, vocabulary, comprehension, and five supported discussion questions about movies and streaming.

Students use a 10-point budget to redesign a local cinema, calculate a ticket price, pitch their idea, and write a short opinion about the future of cinemas.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Not long ago, watching a new movie meant leaving the house. You checked the times, went across town, bought a ticket and sat in the dark with strangers. Today the same movie comes to your sofa, on a big screen, for less than the price of one ticket.
