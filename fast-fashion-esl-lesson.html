ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Fast Fashion ESL Lesson

Fast fashion has made it possible to buy new clothes for remarkably low prices. Stores constantly introduce new styles, encouraging shoppers to buy more often and treat clothing as something temporary rather than something designed to last.

This free B1–B2 Fast Fashion ESL lesson explores the hidden environmental and human costs behind cheap clothing. Students consider overconsumption, the environmental impact of the fashion industry and whether consumers, clothing companies or governments should take more responsibility for making fashion sustainable.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss fast fashion, cheap clothing, shopping habits, ethical fashion, sustainability and the real cost behind inexpensive clothes.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A brand-new T-shirt for the price of a coffee sounds like a bargain. In fast-fashion stores, shelves are refilled with cheap new garments almost every week, and shoppers are encouraged to buy far more than they need. But behind that five-dollar price tag lies a hidden cost that never appears on the receipt.
