Decline of Reading ESL Discussion Questions

15 conversation questions about books, screens, skimming, deep reading and whether the way we read is changing rather than disappearing.
A1–A2 BeginnerB1–B2 IntermediateC1–C2 Advanced

Use these decline of reading ESL discussion questions for pair work, small-group speaking or whole-class conversation. Choose the level that fits your learners, or move through all three sections as the questions become more challenging.

A

Beginner · A1–A2

Start the conversation

  1. 01

    Do you read for pleasure? What do you read?

  2. 02

    What was your favorite book or story as a child?

  3. 03

    Why do you think people finish fewer books today?

  4. 04

    Is reading a long post the same as reading a book?

  5. 05

    Will your children read books, or something else?

B

Intermediate · B1–B2

Explore the issue

  1. 01

    What was the last book you read for pleasure, not for work or school?

  2. 02

    Has your own reading changed since you were younger? How?

  3. 03

    Why might long books be harder to finish today than in the past?

  4. 04

    Is reading a comic or scrolling social media really “reading”?

  5. 05

    Are we giving ourselves enough time to think deeply about anything?

Do we still read, or only scan?

One topic. Three levels. Countless perspectives.
C

Advanced · C1–C2

Challenge the idea

  1. 01

    Do you read differently on a screen than on paper? How?

  2. 02

    Is skimming a weakness, or a sensible response to too much information?

  3. 03

    Why might a society of skimmers be easier to persuade?

  4. 04

    Should schools require long books, even if students resist them?

  5. 05

    If stories move to audio and video, has anything important been lost?
