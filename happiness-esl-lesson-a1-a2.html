ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Beginner Happiness and Money ESL Lesson

A man finally buys the car he has wanted for years. At first, every drive feels exciting. A few weeks later, the feeling begins to disappear and the car becomes part of everyday life.

This A1–A2 lesson uses accessible English to explore money, happiness and spending. Students consider why people quickly get used to new things, what they may give up to earn more money, and whether experiences can make us happy for longer than possessions.

Students compare five ways to spend $200, decide how long the happy feeling might last, plan a happy week for a tired friend, and discuss how much money is enough for a happy life.
