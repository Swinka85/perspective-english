Finding suitable B1 and B2 ESL lessons can be difficult. Intermediate learners need accessible language, but they also need topics sophisticated enough to generate real opinions and extended conversation.

Perspective English lessons are designed primarily for B1–B2 adult and university learners. Instead of isolated grammar exercises, students use English to explore questions about technology, society, work, money, media, culture, food, sport, and the environment.

Each lesson begins with an original article and develops into vocabulary, comprehension, discussion, critical thinking, and speaking. Complete issues also include audio and teacher support, making them suitable for classroom teaching, tutoring, and conversation courses.

CEFR B1

Intermediate ESL Lessons

B1 learners can understand the main points of clear texts on familiar subjects and communicate opinions about everyday topics. Perspective lessons give intermediate students structured support while encouraging them to move beyond short answers.

CEFR B2

Upper-Intermediate ESL Lessons

B2 learners can engage with more complex ideas, explain viewpoints, compare arguments, and participate more spontaneously in discussion. Perspective activities encourage students to analyze issues, defend choices, and respond to different perspectives.

What's included in a Perspective ESL lesson?

Perspective lessons are designed as complete B1–B2 classroom resources. Rather than giving teachers a single article or list of questions, each issue develops one contemporary topic through a sequence of reading, language, discussion, and critical-thinking activities.

Reading & Vocabulary

Original B1–B2 articles with useful vocabulary and comprehension tasks.

Discussion

Open questions designed to generate opinions rather than one-word answers.

Critical Thinking

Ranking, decision-making, analysis, comparison, and problem-solving activities.

Teacher Resources

Complete issues include audio, answer support, teacher guidance, and presentation materials.
