ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Pokémon ESL Lesson

Pokémon began as a simple video game in Japan and grew into one of the world's biggest entertainment franchises. For more than three decades, its games, trading cards, television series, films and mobile apps have attracted fans across generations.

This B1–B2 Pokémon ESL lesson explores how Pokémon became a global cultural phenomenon. Students examine the roles of collecting, nostalgia, memorable characters, branding, technological innovation and fan communities in the franchise's lasting success.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss gaming, popular culture, global brands, fandom, nostalgia, mobile gaming and why some entertainment franchises remain successful for decades.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Pokémon began in Japan in 1996 as a game built around a simple idea: explore, collect, trade, and battle. That formula quickly expanded beyond video games into television, films, trading cards, toys, and mobile apps, turning Pokémon into a global entertainment phenomenon.
