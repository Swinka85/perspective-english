ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Looksmaxxing ESL Lesson

Looksmaxxing turns appearance into an optimization project, ranging from ordinary grooming and exercise to fillers, injections and surgery.

This C1–C2 Looksmaxxing ESL lesson examines beauty pressure, algorithms, cosmetic-industry incentives and the point at which self-improvement may become self-rejection.

Students work with advanced idiomatic language, evaluate six appearance-related choices, defend a policy proposal for teenage beauty content and write an extended argument addressing an opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Wanting to be attractive is as old as the mirror itself. So why does a young man measuring the angle of his own jaw with an app strike so many people as a different story altogether?
