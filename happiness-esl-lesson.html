ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Happiness ESL Lesson

People often chase happiness through higher salaries, successful careers, comfortable lifestyles, exciting experiences and material possessions. However, every choice can carry a hidden cost.

This B1–B2 Happiness ESL lesson explores the trade-offs people make in pursuit of a better life. Students consider whether money can improve well-being and whether success is worth sacrificing time, sleep, health, relationships, privacy or personal freedom.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss happiness, priorities, ambition, work-life balance, money, success and what it means to live a good life.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Happiness is often presented as something we can earn, buy, or achieve. Yet the pursuit of a better life can quietly cost us time, sleep, health, relationships, privacy, and freedom. The question is not only what makes us happy, but what we are willing to trade for it.
