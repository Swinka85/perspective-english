The Side Hustle

Is a second job a path to freedom or another source of pressure?

LESSON PREVIEW

ABOUT THIS LESSON

For many people, one job is no longer enough. Some start a side hustle to earn extra money, develop new skills, test a business idea, or gain more control over their future.

This magazine-style English lesson explores both sides of the side hustle economy. It examines the appeal of flexible work and entrepreneurship, while also considering unstable income, long working hours, burnout, and the pressure to turn every hobby into profit.

Students read an original article, learn useful vocabulary, and take part in discussion, critical-thinking, speaking, and writing activities about money, ambition, entrepreneurship, job security, personal time, and work-life balance.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A side hustle can offer extra income, new skills, and a possible route out of an unsatisfying job. But it can also turn evenings and weekends into more working hours. For some people it represents freedom; for others, it is evidence that one salary is no longer enough.
