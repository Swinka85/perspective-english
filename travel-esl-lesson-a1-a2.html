ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Travel ESL Lesson

Two friends visit Thailand. One stays at a resort, while the other explores markets and local places. Students discuss whether both trips count as really experiencing the country.

This A1–A2 lesson uses an accessible article, audio, five vocabulary words, true-or-false comprehension questions and supported discussion about vacations and travel.

Students plan seven days in Thailand with a $700 budget, practice key words in context, present a four-day itinerary for a visitor and write a short opinion about resort vacations.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Two friends fly to Phuket, in Thailand, in the same week. One stays seven days inside a big resort: pool, buffet and spa. The other rents a scooter, eats at a night market, takes a boat and gets lost twice.
