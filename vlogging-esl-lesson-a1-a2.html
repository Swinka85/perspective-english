ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Vlogging ESL Lesson

Millions of people watch strangers cook, clean, work and talk online. These simple videos can feel friendly and relaxing, but creators may also feel pressure to share more of their private lives.

This A1–A2 Vlogging ESL lesson helps beginner learners talk about daily videos, online creators, loneliness, honesty, privacy and what should stay off camera.

Students use a short original reading and audio, beginner vocabulary, true-or-false comprehension, five discussion questions, a privacy-ranking activity, a one-year vlogging speaking challenge and guided writing.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A woman fries an egg. She does not speak. She cleans the kitchen, waters a plant and folds a blanket. Then the video ends. It has forty million views.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

What's Included in the Vlogging Lesson?

  • Magazine-style A1–A2 ESL student worksheet
  • Short original vlogging reading and audio recording
  • Five beginner vocabulary items and true-or-false reading comprehension questions
  • Five supported discussion questions about daily videos, creators, privacy, honesty and children online
  • Would You Film It? critical-thinking activity: rank eight personal moments from happy to film to never film
  • Discuss where students would stop filming and why
  • Language in Context practice with words from the article
  • One-year vlogging speaking challenge: create three rules, decide who must be asked first and explain what would make you stop
  • 60–100 word guided writing task: When people are paid to share their lives, is it still real life?
  • Teacher guide and complete answer key
  • Ready-to-use classroom presentation
