ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Driving on the Left or Right ESL Lesson

Why do some countries drive on the left and others on the right? This A1–A2 lesson explores the history of road rules, from old ideas about horse riders and wagons to modern traffic.

Students discover why Britain and countries such as India and Australia drive on the left, consider a possible explanation for Japan’s rule, and learn how Sweden switched to right-side driving on Dagen H.

Students discuss driving habits and cultural differences, trace the ripple effects of a national road-rule change, role-play a radio interview with a government minister, and write about whether everyday rules should be the same around the world.
