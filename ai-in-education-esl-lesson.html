ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This AI in Education ESL Lesson

Artificial intelligence is rapidly becoming part of education. Students can now use AI tools to explain difficult ideas, answer questions, improve writing and provide personalized support at almost any time of day.

This free B1–B2 AI in Education ESL lesson explores how artificial intelligence could change the way people learn. Students consider the possibilities of AI tutors and personalized learning while discussing what could happen to the traditional role of teachers as educational technology becomes more powerful.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss artificial intelligence, AI tutors, personalized learning, education technology and whether machines can genuinely help people become better learners.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Imagine a classroom where every student has a private tutor, available at any hour, ready to explain a difficult idea in a dozen different ways. For a growing number of learners, this is no longer science fiction. Artificial intelligence has entered education, and it is changing how people study, write, and even think.
