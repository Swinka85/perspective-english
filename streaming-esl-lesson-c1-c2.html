ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Streaming & Cinema ESL Lesson

Streaming offers endless choice and convenience, yet it competes for our attention. Meanwhile, cinemas face expensive tickets, inconvenient showtimes and an audience that often praises the experience without buying tickets.

This C1–C2 lesson explores choice overload, attention, consumer behavior and the tension between what audiences say they value and what they actually do.

Students debate the future of cinema, design a business model within a 10-point budget, defend it against a streaming rival and write an argument that addresses the strongest opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Not long ago, seeing a new film required a small expedition. You checked the listings, crossed town, paid at a window and sat in the dark among strangers. Today the same film arrives on the sofa, on a screen larger than the one your parents grew up with, for less than the price of a single ticket.
