ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Vlogging ESL Lesson

Millions of people now watch strangers cook breakfast, commute to work, shop for groceries, clean their homes, travel, study and talk to a camera. Vlogging has turned everyday life into entertainment, and some content creators build enormous audiences without doing anything traditionally extraordinary.

This B1–B2 Vlogging ESL lesson explores why watching ordinary people can feel personal, relaxing and more authentic than polished entertainment. Students also consider what happens when everyday life becomes content and the boundaries between public and private begin to disappear.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss vloggers, livestreaming, social media, online privacy, authenticity, parasocial relationships and why people choose to broadcast their lives.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Millions of people now watch strangers cook breakfast, commute to work, shop for groceries, clean their homes, travel and simply talk to a camera. Vlogging has turned ordinary life into entertainment. But why do we want strangers to watch our lives, and why do so many people want to watch?
