ABOUT PERSPECTIVE ENGLISH

Lessons worth talking about.

Perspective English creates magazine-style ESL lessons that help learners explore meaningful ideas, develop critical thinking skills, and communicate with greater confidence.

OUR STORY

English lessons should create real conversations.

Perspective English was created with one simple goal: to help teachers spend less time preparing materials and more time engaging their students.

Every issue is built around a current topic, interesting question, or global trend that gives learners something meaningful to read, consider, and discuss.

Rather than relying on isolated grammar exercises, Perspective combines magazine-style reading with practical classroom activities designed to encourage discussion, collaboration, and thoughtful communication.

THE PERSPECTIVE APPROACH

Language, ideas, and discussion.

Whether the topic is technology, culture, sport, science, business, society, or world events, each lesson aims to develop more than language knowledge alone.

ENGAGING CONTENT

Topics that matter

Lessons begin with relevant questions and modern topics that students can connect to their own experiences and opinions.

CRITICAL THINKING

Ideas from different angles

Activities encourage learners to analyze information, consider different viewpoints, solve problems, and support their ideas.

REAL COMMUNICATION

Confidence through conversation

Speaking tasks give students a clear reason to exchange ideas, make decisions, collaborate, and communicate in English.

WHAT IS INCLUDED?

Complete lessons with minimal preparation.

Perspective lessons are designed to give teachers everything they need to take an engaging topic directly into the classroom.

  • Original magazine-style reading articles
  • Warm-up and pre-reading questions
  • Useful vocabulary activities
  • Reading comprehension exercises
  • Discussion questions
  • Critical thinking activities
  • Speaking challenges
  • Teacher guides and answer keys
  • Classroom presentations
  • Audio versions of each article

WHO ARE THE LESSONS FOR?

Flexible materials for communication-focused classes.

Perspective English is designed primarily for university English courses, adult learners, conversation classes, discussion programs, and courses that focus on communication and critical thinking.

Most lessons are suitable for learners around the B1–B2 level, although teachers can adapt the activities for different classes, abilities, and teaching contexts.

WHY PERSPECTIVE?

Every topic has more than one side.

Perspective lessons begin with interesting questions rather than simple answers.

The goal is not only to practice English, but also to encourage learners to explore different viewpoints, explain their reasoning, listen to others, and discuss ideas that matter.

Because the best English lessons do more than teach language. They create conversations.

OUR MISSION

Ready to teach. Worth discussing.

Our mission is to create beautifully designed, magazine-style ESL lessons that teachers can open and teach with confidence, combining engaging content with practical materials that save preparation time without sacrificing quality.
