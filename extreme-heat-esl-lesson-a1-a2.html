ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Extreme Heat ESL Lesson

Extreme heat is becoming part of everyday life in more places. Hotter days and warmer nights can affect health, work and the way cities function.

This A1–A2 Extreme Heat ESL lesson helps beginner learners talk about heatwaves, dangerous temperatures, air conditioning, climate change and ways to protect people from extreme heat.

Students use a short original reading and audio, beginner vocabulary, true-or-false comprehension, five discussion questions, a city-budget critical-thinking activity, a mayor speaking challenge and guided writing.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Every summer, a hospital in one desert city sees strange patients: people who fell on the sidewalk. In the sun, the ground can reach 82°C, hot enough to burn skin in seconds. Once, this was big news. Now the city plans for it.
