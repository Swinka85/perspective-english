ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Vlogging ESL Lesson

Ordinary routines now attract enormous online audiences. Parasocial bonds can provide company, while creators can earn serious money by turning private life into public content.

This C1–C2 Vlogging ESL lesson examines authenticity, loneliness, privacy, creator culture and the pressure to reveal more when income depends on attention.

Students work with advanced expressions, rank the moments they would be willing to film, defend a privacy rule against a sponsor and write an extended argument addressing the strongest opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A woman fries an egg. She does not speak. She wipes the counter, waters a plant, folds a blanket, and the video ends. It has forty million views. Somewhere in the comments, a stranger explains that they play it every night because the apartment sounds like their grandmother’s.
