ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This American Politics ESL Lesson

What happens when family members support different political parties? In this beginner-friendly lesson, students meet a family whose political differences threaten to interrupt Thanksgiving dinner.

Students learn the colors and symbols of the Democratic and Republican parties, explore broad differences in their views and consider whether people can disagree while staying close.

The lesson includes house-rule decision-making, a family role-play and a short opinion-writing task about discussing politics at dinner.
