ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Beginner Women’s Sport ESL Lesson

Women’s sport is attracting bigger crowds, larger television audiences and more attention than ever before. Famous players and major tournaments are helping new fans discover women’s teams and competitions.

This A1–A2 lesson uses accessible English to explore the growth of women’s sport, why audiences are increasing and why questions about support, prize money and equality still matter.

Students compare situations in men’s and women’s sport, discuss fairness and support, and defend their choices in a speaking challenge.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Women’s sport is becoming more popular around the world. Bigger crowds watch matches, famous athletes have millions of fans, and more people are asking whether men and women should receive equal support.
