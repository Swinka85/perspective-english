Life Skills & Education ESL Discussion Questions

15 conversation questions about practical life skills, traditional school subjects, and whether education prepares us for the real world.
A1–A2 BeginnerB1–B2 IntermediateC1–C2 Advanced

Use these life-skills ESL discussion questions for pair work, small-group speaking or whole-class conversation. Explore what schools teach, what students need for adult life, and the difficult choices involved in changing the curriculum. Choose the level that fits your learners.

A

Beginner · A1–A2

Start the conversation

  1. 01

    Which subject did you like most at school? Has it been useful?

  2. 02

    Did you ever need to know something that nobody taught you?

  3. 03

    Why do you think schools change their subjects so slowly?

  4. 04

    Is a subject useful if you forget the facts but keep the thinking skills?

  5. 05

    What is school really for: life, work, knowledge or thinking?

B

Intermediate · B1–B2

Explore the issue

  1. 01

    Which subject did you enjoy most at school, and has it been useful?

  2. 02

    Was there something you badly needed to know and nobody taught you?

  3. 03

    Why do you think schools change their subjects so slowly?

  4. 04

    Is a subject worth keeping if students forget the content but keep the thinking?

  5. 05

    What is school actually for: adult life, employment, knowledge, or thinking?

What should schools teach, and what should they stop teaching?

One topic. Three levels. Countless perspectives.
C

Advanced · C1–C2

Challenge the idea

  1. 01

    Which subject from school has quietly shaped how you think as an adult?

  2. 02

    What did you learn the hard way that school could have taught you?

  3. 03

    Why might governments promise practical subjects but rarely deliver them?

  4. 04

    Can a subject earn its place if students forget almost all of its content?

  5. 05

    Should school prepare people for jobs, for life, or for thinking itself?
