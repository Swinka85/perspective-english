ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Extreme Heat ESL Lesson

Extreme heat is becoming increasingly normal in cities around the world. Rising temperatures affect health, work and infrastructure, while adaptation can reduce immediate harm without addressing the underlying cause.

This C1–C2 Extreme Heat ESL lesson examines dangerous heat, climate adaptation, prevention and the tension between protecting people now and reducing future warming.

Students work with advanced expressions, weigh a fixed city budget across competing heat measures, defend an unpopular policy under challenge and write an extended argument addressing the strongest opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

In one desert city, the summer burn unit fills with an unusual kind of patient: people who fell onto the sidewalk. Asphalt in direct sun can reach 82°C, hot enough to burn skin within seconds. Such heat was once called freak weather. Today the city budgets for it, warns visitors and, like its residents, takes it in stride.
