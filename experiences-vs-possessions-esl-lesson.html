ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Experiences vs Possessions ESL Lesson

For generations, success was often measured by the things people owned: a home, a car, fashionable clothes, electronics and other visible signs of wealth. Today, many consumers say they would rather spend their money on experiences.

This B1–B2 Experiences vs Possessions ESL lesson explores whether travel, concerts, restaurants, festivals, wellness retreats and once-in-a-lifetime activities really create more happiness than material possessions. Students also consider what happens when businesses learn to package and sell experiences just like physical products.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss happiness, spending, memories, status, social media, authenticity and modern consumer culture.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Travel, concerts, restaurants and once-in-a-lifetime experiences can create memories that last for years. But as companies become better at packaging and selling those moments, experiences can start to look surprisingly similar to the possessions people once wanted to escape.
