ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Fake Reviews ESL Lesson

Online reviews help people choose what to buy, where to eat and what to watch. But some reviews are written by people who were paid, given free products or never used the product at all.

This A1–A2 Fake Reviews ESL lesson uses clear language to help beginner learners talk about trust, online reviews and simple ways to check whether a review looks honest.

The lesson combines a short original reading and audio with beginner vocabulary, true-or-false comprehension, five discussion questions, a Spot the Fake activity, speaking practice and guided writing.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Fake reviews are now a big business. Some companies pay people to write five-star reviews. Others write bad reviews about the shop next door. Some sellers give shoppers a free product if they write something nice.
