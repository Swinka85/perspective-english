ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Space Tourism ESL Lesson

For centuries, traveling into space was impossible for ordinary people. Even after human spaceflight became a reality, the experience remained limited almost entirely to highly trained astronauts.

This B1–B2 Space Tourism ESL lesson explores how commercial spaceflight is beginning to change that. Students consider private space travel, the high price of a ticket and whether access to space should remain an experience available mainly to the wealthy.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to debate the benefits and disadvantages of space tourism and consider how commercial space travel could develop in the future.

FROM THE ARTICLE

For most of history, only trained astronauts left Earth. Now, anyone with enough money can buy a ticket. Private companies launch paying customers into the sky, and for a few minutes they float in weightlessness and see the curve of the planet below.
