LESSON PREVIEW

ABOUT THIS LESSON

Phones help us communicate, work and stay informed, but many people feel they are spending too much time looking at screens.

This magazine-style English lesson introduces the idea of a digital detox and encourages students to examine their own relationship with technology.

Students read an original article, learn useful vocabulary and discuss whether switching off can improve our lives.

FROM THE ARTICLE

For many people, the first thing they see in the morning is a phone screen. Messages, news updates and social media notifications can begin before they have even left bed. Technology has made life faster and more convenient, but it has also made switching off increasingly difficult.
