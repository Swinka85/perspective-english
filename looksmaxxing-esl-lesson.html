Looksmaxxing

How far should we go to look better?

A ready-to-teach English lesson exploring looksmaxxing, beauty standards, appearance pressure, self-improvement, and how social media influences the way people see themselves.

LESSON PREVIEW

ABOUT THIS LESSON

People have always tried to improve their appearance, from changing hairstyles and clothing to exercising, dieting, and using cosmetics. But online culture has pushed self-improvement in a new direction.

Looksmaxxing encourages people to analyze and optimize their appearance, sometimes through simple lifestyle changes and sometimes through more extreme methods. Social media, beauty filters, online rankings, and constant comparison can make physical appearance feel like a problem that always needs fixing.

This magazine-style English lesson explores beauty standards, appearance pressure, self-improvement, and the influence of online culture. Students read an original article and take part in vocabulary, discussion, critical-thinking, speaking, and writing activities.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Improving your appearance may begin as a personal choice. But when every feature can be measured, ranked, and compared, self-improvement can quickly become something very different.
