Looksmaxxing

How far should we go to look better?

LESSON PREVIEW

ABOUT THIS LESSON

People have always tried to improve their appearance, but social media has turned self-improvement into something more intense. For some, looking better is no longer just about style or fitness. It becomes a project.

Looksmaxxing can include skincare, exercise, hairstyles, fashion, cosmetic treatments, and even surgery. Supporters see it as self-improvement and confidence-building, while critics worry that it can create unrealistic standards and make people constantly dissatisfied with themselves.

This magazine-style English lesson explores appearance pressure, beauty standards, social media, self-image, and how far people should go to look better. Students read an original article and take part in vocabulary, discussion, critical-thinking, speaking, and writing activities.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Improving your appearance can make you feel more confident. But when every feature becomes something to measure, compare, and fix, self-improvement can begin to feel like a competition you can never finish.
