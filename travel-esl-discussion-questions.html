Travel & Tourism ESL Discussion Questions

15 conversation questions about resort holidays, exploring unfamiliar places, local communities and what makes a journey meaningful.
A1–A2 BeginnerB1–B2 IntermediateC1–C2 Advanced

Use these travel ESL discussion questions for pair work, small-group speaking or whole-class conversation. Compare resort holidays and independent travel, explore the impact of tourism on local communities, and ask what it really means to experience a country. Choose the level that fits your learners.

A

Beginner · A1–A2

Start the conversation

  1. 01

    Are you more like the resort friend or the scooter friend?

  2. 02

    Have you visited a place and felt you never really saw it?

  3. 03

    Why do some people prefer to stay inside the resort?

  4. 04

    Should tourists spend money in local shops and restaurants?

  5. 05

    Is there a wrong way to spend your own vacation?

B

Intermediate · B1–B2

Explore the issue

  1. 01

    Which kind of traveler are you closer to: the resort or the scooter?

  2. 02

    Have you been somewhere and felt you never really saw the country?

  3. 03

    Why do some travelers prefer the resort to the country outside it?

  4. 04

    Does a tourist have any responsibility to the place they visit?

  5. 05

    Is there any wrong way to spend your own holiday?

C

Advanced · C1–C2

Challenge the idea

  1. 01

    Which traveler are you closer to: the resort guest or the scooter rider?

  2. 02

    Have you visited somewhere and felt you only scratched the surface?

  3. 03

    Why might a resort feel more appealing than the country outside it?

  4. 04

    Does a tourist owe anything to the economy of the place they visit?

  5. 05

    Can there be a wrong way to spend your own vacation?
