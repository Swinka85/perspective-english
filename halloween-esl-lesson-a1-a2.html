ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Halloween ESL Lesson

Why do people dress up, carve pumpkins and ask strangers for candy on Halloween? This beginner-friendly A1–A2 ESL lesson introduces the sights and customs of Halloween and explores where the celebration came from.

Students read about the ancient festival of Samhain in Ireland and Scotland, European immigrants bringing customs to North America, the shift from carved turnips to pumpkins, and how American movies and TV helped spread Halloween worldwide.

Students discuss costumes, scary stories and traditions, check five claims about Halloween, negotiate rules for a neighborhood Halloween party, and write about festivals from other countries.
