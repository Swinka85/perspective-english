ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Harry Potter ESL Lesson

The Sorting Hat divides Hogwarts students into four houses according to character, creating an immediate sense of belonging while also placing each child inside a powerful label.

This C1–C2 Harry Potter ESL lesson uses that familiar idea to examine identity, group membership, rivalry and individual choice. Students consider why being sorted can make people feel understood and whether belonging to a group can also put people in a box.

The lesson develops advanced discussion and argumentation through idiomatic vocabulary, evidence-based comprehension, evaluation of the groups that shape identity, a school-policy challenge and an extended opinion-writing task.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Supporters argue that such labels help people fit in and find others who share their values, whether in a sports club, a company or a group of friends. Critics warn that when people are put in a box, a complicated person is reduced to one word, and groups turn into rivals.
