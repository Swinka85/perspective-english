ISSUE 026

Fake Reviews

Can you trust what you read online?

A ready-to-teach English lesson exploring fake reviews, online ratings, consumer trust, and how easily opinions can be manipulated online.

Buy Lesson Listen to Audio

LESSON PREVIEW

ABOUT THIS LESSON

Before choosing a restaurant, hotel, product, or service, many people check the reviews first. A high rating can quickly build trust, while a few negative comments can make customers look elsewhere.

But online reviews are not always genuine. Businesses can buy positive ratings, competitors can post negative ones, and ordinary users may struggle to know which opinions are real.

This magazine-style English lesson explores fake reviews, consumer trust, online manipulation, and how people decide what to believe. Students read an original article and take part in vocabulary, discussion, critical-thinking, speaking, and writing activities.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Reviews were supposed to help us make better decisions. But when ratings can be bought, copied, or manipulated, the real challenge may be deciding which voices deserve our trust.
