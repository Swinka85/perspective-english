ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Future of Work ESL Lesson

Artificial intelligence is already changing the workplace, completing tasks that once required human workers and raising questions about which jobs may disappear, change or be created.

This B1–B2 Future of Work ESL lesson explores how AI, automation and new technology are transforming jobs and careers. Students consider which professions may be most affected and which human skills could become more valuable as technology improves.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss employment, automation, career choices, workplace skills and how artificial intelligence could shape the future of work.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Artificial intelligence is already writing emails, creating images, analyzing data, and helping doctors make decisions. As technology continues to improve, one question is becoming more important than ever: which jobs will still need people?
