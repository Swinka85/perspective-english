Privacy Policy

How Perspective English uses information across its website, teaching resources and interactive Practice Rooms.

1. Who we are

Perspective English provides English-language teaching resources and free interactive Practice Rooms at perspectiveenglish.com. For privacy enquiries, please use the contact information on our About page. Before publication: add a direct privacy contact email or verified contact form link here.

2. Information collected when you visit

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies and process technical information about your browser, device, and approximate location. The precise data collected depends on our analytics configuration and your browser settings. We do not intend to use Analytics reports to identify individual visitors.

3. Interactive Practice Rooms and voting

Our Practice Rooms offer Solo Practice and Classroom Mode. Some activities include an optional “What the World Thinks” voting feature, powered by Google Firebase, which displays combined responses from participants. If you choose to vote, your selection is sent to Firebase so that aggregate results can be calculated and displayed. Verification required before publication: confirm whether Firebase also records timestamps, IP addresses, authentication or installation identifiers, abuse-prevention information, or other technical metadata; identify the Firebase products in use, retention rules, database region and security settings. We must not describe this feature as fully anonymous until those checks are complete.

4. Purchases and payments

When you buy a teaching resource, the checkout and delivery process is handled through Payhip and its supported payment providers. They may collect information needed to complete the transaction, such as your name, email address, billing information and payment details, under their own privacy policies. Perspective English may receive order and customer details needed to fulfil purchases and provide support. We do not ask you to enter payment-card information into our Practice Rooms.

5. Cookies and third-party services

Google Analytics and embedded third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. Your browser may allow you to restrict or delete cookies. Before publication: check which cookies and services are actually loaded on the live website, whether consent controls are required for visitors in applicable jurisdictions, and whether analytics is disabled until consent where required.

6. Why information is used

We use information to operate the website and Practice Rooms, provide purchased resources, respond to enquiries, understand website performance, maintain security and display optional aggregated voting results. The lawful basis for each use depends on the processing and applicable law; it should be confirmed as part of the final privacy review.

7. Sharing and international processing

Relevant information may be processed by service providers such as Google (Analytics and Firebase), Payhip and payment processors. These providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their terms and privacy notices provide more detail about their practices. Before publication: verify the actual providers, processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards.

8. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, subject to legal and operational requirements. Purchase records may need to be retained for accounting or tax purposes. Before publication: insert verified retention periods or criteria for Firebase votes, analytics data, and any contact records.

9. Your choices and rights

You can use Practice Rooms without participating in the optional voting feature. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, object to certain processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us through our published privacy contact method to make a request. Rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction.

10. Children and students

Practice Rooms may be used in educational settings. Teachers and schools should consider their own responsibilities when using online tools with students. Before publication: confirm whether the site is intended for children, whether student information is collected, and whether any additional notices or consents are needed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and services change. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
