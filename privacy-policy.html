Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

How Perspective English uses information across its website, teaching resources and interactive Practice Rooms.

Effective date: 7 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Perspective English provides English-language teaching resources and free interactive Practice Rooms at perspectiveenglish.com. For privacy enquiries and requests about your information, please use the contact details on our About page.

2. Information collected when you visit

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies and process technical information about your browser, device, and approximate location. The precise data collected depends on our analytics configuration and your browser settings. We do not intend to use Analytics reports to identify individual visitors.

3. Interactive Practice Rooms and voting

Our Practice Rooms offer Solo Practice and Classroom Mode. Selected critical-thinking activities include “What the World Thinks”, a feature powered by Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) that shows combined responses from participating visitors and classes.

In the voting implementation reviewed, Perspective English sends a Practice Room identifier and increments aggregate vote counters. Names, email addresses and account identifiers are not included in the voting payload. The browser also stores information locally, including voting timestamps used to limit repeat submissions and a preferred practice mode. These local settings may remain until cleared through your browser.

Submitting the result of a voting-enabled activity may also submit its vote automatically; there is not a separate vote opt-in for that submission. You can choose not to submit the result. The displayed totals are combined counts rather than individual response histories.

Although the voting payload does not deliberately include directly identifying details, Google Firebase may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, as part of providing and securing the service. We therefore do not claim that all processing is fully anonymous. The Firestore database used for aggregate vote counts is located in Seoul, South Korea (asia-northeast3). Other technical processing by Google may take place elsewhere.

4. Purchases and payments

When you buy a teaching resource, the checkout and delivery process is handled through Payhip and its supported payment providers. They may collect information needed to complete the transaction, such as your name, email address, billing information and payment details, under their own privacy policies. Perspective English may receive order and customer details needed to fulfil purchases and provide support. We do not ask you to enter payment-card information into our Practice Rooms.

5. Cookies and third-party services

Google Analytics and embedded third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. Practice Rooms may also use browser storage to remember practice preferences and help limit repeat voting. Your browser may allow you to restrict or delete cookies and locally stored information.

6. Why information is used

We use information to operate the website and Practice Rooms, provide purchased resources, respond to enquiries, understand website performance, maintain security and display aggregated voting results. Depending on applicable law, these activities may rely on legitimate interests, contractual necessity, consent, or legal obligations.

7. Sharing and international processing

Relevant information may be processed by service providers such as Google (Analytics and Firebase), Payhip and payment processors. These providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their terms and privacy notices provide more detail about their practices.

8. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, subject to legal and operational requirements. Aggregate vote counters are kept to provide ongoing worldwide comparisons and may remain until Perspective English deletes them. These counters do not contain individual voting histories. Analytics and transaction records are retained according to applicable service settings and legal or operational requirements. Purchase records may need to be retained for accounting or tax purposes.

9. Your choices and rights

You can use the Practice Rooms without submitting results for voting-enabled activities. Where voting is enabled, submitting an activity result may also send a vote; a separate vote opt-in is not currently provided. You can clear locally stored voting timestamps and preferences through your browser settings, although doing so does not remove votes already counted in Firestore.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, object to certain processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us through our published privacy contact method to make a request. Rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction.

10. Children and students

Practice Rooms may be used in educational settings. Teachers and schools should consider their own responsibilities when using online tools with students. We do not ask students to provide names or email addresses to use Practice Rooms. Teachers should supervise use by children and follow their institution’s policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and services change. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
