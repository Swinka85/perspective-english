ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Food Fraud ESL Lesson

Food is not always what the label says. Honey can contain cheap sugar syrup, good olive oil can be mixed with cheaper oil, and some products can be sold under a false name.

This A1–A2 Food Fraud ESL lesson uses clear language to help beginner learners talk about food labels, fake products, prices and trust. Students consider who should stop food fraud and what should happen when companies lie about food.

The lesson combines a short original reading and audio with beginner vocabulary, true-or-false comprehension, discussion, a simple judgment activity, speaking practice and guided writing.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Food fraud comes in many forms. Some companies mix good olive oil with cheaper oil. Some sell fake wine, or give a fish a new name to sell it for more money. The food changes, but the idea is the same: someone makes money, and the shopper does not know.
