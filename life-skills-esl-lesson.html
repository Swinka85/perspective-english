ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Life Skills ESL Lesson

Students can spend years learning equations, literature, science and history, yet still leave school unsure how taxes work, how credit grows, what investing means or how to handle many of the practical decisions of adult life.

This B1–B2 Life Skills ESL lesson asks what schools should really teach. Students explore the case for financial literacy, first aid, cooking, AI literacy, basic law and other practical subjects, while also considering why traditional subjects such as mathematics, literature and science still matter.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion and critical-thinking activities. Students redesign the school week, decide what should be added, choose what would lose classroom time, and debate the bigger question: what is school actually for?

FROM THE ARTICLE

Students leave school knowing how to solve equations, analyze texts and explain scientific ideas, but many know far less about taxes, investing, credit or other parts of adult life. If schools add more practical subjects, however, something else has to lose classroom time. So what should education really prepare us for?
