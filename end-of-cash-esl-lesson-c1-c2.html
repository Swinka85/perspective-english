ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Advanced Cashless Society ESL Lesson

Paying by card or phone can feel effortless, but the disappearance of cash raises questions that go far beyond convenience.

This C1–C2 lesson examines who benefits financially from digital payments and who may lose access, privacy or independence. Students consider the interests of banks, businesses and governments alongside the experiences of older people, the unbanked and people who depend on cash as a safety net.

Students evaluate who wins and loses from a fully digital payment system before advising a government on one rule for a ten-year transition away from cash and defending that policy against opposition.

FROM THE ARTICLE

The benefits are real. Digital payments are fast, and they leave a record, which makes tax evasion and money laundering harder. For businesses, handling notes is costly and risky; for banks and card companies, every tap earns a small fee.
