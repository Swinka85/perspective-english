Neighborhood Change: ESL Discussion Questions

15 classroom conversation questions about changing neighborhoods, disappearing local shops, urban development, neighbors, and what makes a place feel like home.
A1–A2 BeginnerB1–B2 IntermediateC1–C2 Advanced

Are our neighborhoods getting better, or losing what made them special? Explore community change through 15 ESL discussion questions from Perspective English Issue 053, “No Place Like Home!” Students discuss familiar places, redevelopment, loneliness, and belonging at three English levels. Suitable for pair work, group discussion, and classroom debates.

A

Beginner · A1–A2

Start the conversation

  1. 01

    What is your favorite place in your neighborhood? Why?

  2. 02

    Has a shop or café you loved ever closed? How did you feel?

  3. 03

    Why can people live close together and still not know each other?

  4. 04

    Should cities protect old shops, even if it slows down new building?

  5. 05

    What makes a place feel like home: the buildings, the people or something else?

B

Intermediate · B1–B2

Explore the issue

  1. 01

    What is your favorite place in your neighborhood, and why?

  2. 02

    Has a shop, café or place you loved ever closed? How did you feel?

  3. 03

    Why do you think people can live close together and still not know each other?

  4. 04

    Should cities protect old shops and buildings, even if it slows down development?

  5. 05

    What makes a place feel like home: the buildings, the people, or something else?

C

Advanced · C1–C2

Challenge the idea

  1. 01

    Which place in your neighborhood would you most hate to lose, and why?

  2. 02

    Has a shop or café you loved ever closed? What did the area lose with it?

  3. 03

    Why can people live side by side for years and still not know each other?

  4. 04

    Should cities protect old shops and buildings, even if it slows development?

  5. 05

    What makes a place feel like home: the buildings, the people, or something else?
