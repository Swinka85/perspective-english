ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Pets ESL Lesson

A woman enters a café and sees three dogs. Students explore why dogs are now welcome in so many businesses and why some people disagree.

This beginner-friendly A1–A2 lesson includes a short article and audio, five vocabulary words, five true-or-false questions and supported discussion about pets and public places.

Students make rules for dogs in twelve public places, practice everyday vocabulary, write a café sign and give a short presentation, then complete a guided 60–100 word opinion.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A woman walks into a café. One dog sleeps under a table, another sits by the counter, and a third arrives in a stroller. Some people love this. Others are afraid of dogs or have allergies. Who should decide where pets are welcome?
