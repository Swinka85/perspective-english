ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Beginner Pokémon ESL Lesson

Pokémon began with a simple idea: catch small monsters, train them and collect them all. Thirty years later, the same characters appear across games, cards, television, movies and toys.

This A1–A2 lesson uses accessible English to explore collecting, trading, childhood memories and the reasons games and characters can stay popular across generations.

Students build a launch plan for a new character with a limited budget, decide which ideas work best together, and then advise an older game franchise on how to attract a new generation.

FROM THE ARTICLE

It started with a simple idea: catch small monsters, train them, and collect them all. Thirty years later, that idea is a giant franchise: games, cards, TV shows, movies and toys, all with the same characters.
