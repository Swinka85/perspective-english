9/11 ESL Discussion Questions

15 thoughtful conversation questions about September 11, historical memory, generational perspectives and how an event can shape lives long afterward.
A1–A2 BeginnerB1–B2 IntermediateC1–C2 Advanced

Explore how the September 11 attacks continue to shape everyday life through 15 ESL discussion questions from Perspective English Issue 045, a Special Edition. Students consider memory and history, the changes they have inherited, and how to teach difficult events sensitively. Use these questions for respectful pair work, group discussions or classroom debates at three English levels.

A

Beginner · A1–A2

Start the conversation

  1. 01

    When did you first learn about September 11, and from whom?

  2. 02

    Is there something in your life that you think was always there?

  3. 03

    Why do people who remember an event talk about it differently?

  4. 04

    Should schools show young students the famous pictures of that day?

  5. 05

    Which event today will feel like history to the next generation?

B

Intermediate · B1–B2

Explore the issue

  1. 01

    When did you first learn about September 11, and from whom?

  2. 02

    Is there anything in your daily life you accept simply because it has always been there?

  3. 03

    Why do you think people who remember an event describe it differently from those who study it?

  4. 04

    Should students learn recent history that older people still remember painfully?

  5. 05

    Which event happening now do you think your children will inherit?
