ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This AI in Education ESL Lesson

AI tools can answer questions at any time, explain ideas in different ways and give students extra practice. For many learners, using artificial intelligence is already a normal part of studying.

This free A1–A2 AI in Education ESL lesson gives beginner and elementary learners accessible language for talking about AI and education. Students consider what makes a good teacher, how AI can help with learning and what can happen when people rely on a machine too much.

The lesson includes a short reading and audio, five vocabulary items, true-or-false comprehension, discussion, a critical-thinking activity about acceptable AI use, a speaking challenge and a 60–100 word writing task.
