ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This American Politics ESL Lesson

Why is America so divided into red and blue? This intermediate ESL lesson introduces the Democratic and Republican parties, their different approaches to government, and the way politics becomes part of personal identity.

Students explore how a winner-takes-all electoral system supports two dominant parties, why disagreements can affect families and friendships, and how midterm elections can change the balance of power.

Activities include a wedding-seating moral dilemma, a reporter-and-voter role-play and an opinion-writing task about keeping political views private.
