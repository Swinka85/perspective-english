ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Superstitions ESL Lesson

Why do some hotels skip floor 13, while others avoid floor 4? This beginner A1–A2 lesson introduces superstitions and shows how the same sign can mean good luck in one country and bad luck in another.

Students read and listen to an accessible article about black cats, wedding rain, owls and gifts. They practice five useful words, answer true-or-false questions and discuss lucky numbers, routines and cultural beliefs.

Groups compare six opposite signs, decide which ones would worry them or change their behavior, design a hotel for international guests and write a guided 60–100 word opinion.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A black cat can mean bad luck in some countries and good luck in others. Rain at a wedding is a bad sign for some people, but lucky in Italy. We learn many superstitions from family, and small routines can help us feel calmer.
