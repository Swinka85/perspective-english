ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Side Hustle ESL Lesson

A side hustle is paid work outside a person’s main job. People may teach online, deliver food, sell things or do other work to earn extra money.

This A1–A2 Side Hustle ESL lesson uses clear language to help beginner learners talk about jobs, money, free time and rest. Students consider why people take a second job and whether working more is always a good idea.

The lesson combines a short original reading and audio with vocabulary, true-or-false comprehension, discussion, a simple decision-making activity, speaking practice and guided writing.

FROM THE ARTICLE

A side hustle is paid work outside your main job. People write, teach, drive, or sell old clothes online. Some want extra money. Some want to learn a new skill. Others want a second income in case they lose their first job.
