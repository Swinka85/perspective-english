ABOUT THIS LESSON

Nearly thirty years after Harry Potter first appeared, millions of readers still know exactly which Hogwarts house they belong to. But why do people enjoy being sorted into groups?

This magazine-style English lesson uses the Sorting Hat as a starting point to explore identity, belonging, personality, and labels. It asks whether one category can ever truly describe a person.

Students read an original article, learn useful vocabulary, and take part in discussion, critical-thinking, speaking, and writing activities about Harry Potter, identity, friendship, personality, and belonging.

FROM THE ARTICLE

The Sorting Hat appears to divide students into four houses. Yet perhaps its biggest question is not where Harry belongs, but where all of us feel we belong.
