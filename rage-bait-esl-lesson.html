ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Rage Bait ESL Lesson

Social media is full of content designed to make people laugh, think or connect. But some posts have a very different goal: making people angry enough to react.

This B1–B2 Rage Bait ESL lesson explores how deliberately provocative opinions, videos, headlines and behavior can turn anger into online engagement. Students examine how comments, arguments, shares and replays can help controversial content spread through social media algorithms.

The lesson combines an original reading and audio with vocabulary, comprehension, discussion questions and critical-thinking activities. Students use English to discuss social media, algorithms, outrage, online manipulation, engagement and whether reacting to rage bait actually helps it succeed.

FROM THE ARTICLE

The strangest thing about rage bait is that you do not need to like it for it to succeed. Sometimes your anger is exactly what the person posting it wanted.
