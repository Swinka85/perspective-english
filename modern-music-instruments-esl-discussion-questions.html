Modern Music Instruments ESL Discussion Questions

15 conversation questions about music-making technology, sampling, modern bands and what it really means to be a musician.
A1–A2 BeginnerB1–B2 IntermediateC1–C2 Advanced

Explore how modern music is made with 15 authentic ESL discussion questions from No Strings Attached, Perspective English Issue 041. Discuss learning instruments, producers, sampling, K-pop, music software and whether playing an instrument is essential to being a musician. Use these questions for pair work, group discussions or classroom debates at three English levels.

A

Beginner · A1–A2

Start the conversation

  1. 01

    Have you ever tried to play an instrument? How did it go?

  2. 02

    Is it important to you that a singer can play an instrument?

  3. 03

    Why do people still love groups like K-pop bands?

  4. 04

    Is using a sample from an old song making music or borrowing it?

  5. 05

    Will people still learn the guitar in fifty years? Does it matter?

C

Advanced · C1–C2

Challenge the idea

  1. 01

    Have you ever tried to learn an instrument? What made you stop or keep going?

  2. 02

    Has learning how a song was made ever changed how you felt about it?

  3. 03

    Why do you think audiences still love groups but no longer need a rhythm section?

  4. 04

    Is building a hit around a sample an act of creation or of borrowing?

  5. 05

    If anyone can make a track in ten minutes, is musical skill worth more or less?
