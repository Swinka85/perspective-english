ESL News Lessons & Current Events Worksheets

Explore ready-to-teach ESL news lessons and current events worksheets for B1–B2 English learners. Each lesson uses engaging real-world topics to build reading, vocabulary, speaking, critical thinking, and meaningful classroom discussion.

Browse ESL news lessons → How to teach news articles

Current events give English learners something meaningful to communicate about. Instead of practicing language in isolation, students can use English to examine technology, society, work, culture, media, the environment, and other issues affecting modern life.

Perspective English news lessons and worksheets are designed primarily for B1–B2 adult and university learners. Each complete lesson includes an original reading, useful vocabulary, comprehension questions, discussion, critical thinking, speaking activities, audio, teacher support, and printable classroom materials.

The ESL news lessons and printable current events worksheets below are organized by theme so you can quickly find a topic that fits your class. Many are inspired by stories and debates appearing regularly in the news, while the discussion questions are designed to remain useful long after a particular headline disappears.
