ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Side Hustle ESL Lesson

Side hustles have become a normal part of working life for many people, whether they are trying to make ends meet, build a safety net, develop a neglected skill or escape the daily grind.

This C1–C2 Side Hustle ESL lesson examines the tension between opportunity and overwork. Students consider whether earning money on the side represents ambition and freedom, or whether the trend reflects low wages and a culture of burning the candle at both ends.

The lesson develops advanced discussion and argumentation through idiomatic vocabulary, evidence-based comprehension, critical evaluation of different side hustles, a workplace-policy challenge and an extended opinion-writing task.

FROM THE ARTICLE

For some, the answer is simple: rising rents and grocery bills mean a single salary no longer helps them make ends meet. Others want a safety net, a second income in case their main job disappears. Many are chasing something more personal: a skill they never had time to develop, or an escape from the daily grind.
