ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Fast Food & Globalization ESL Lesson

Global food brands rarely export identical menus. They reshape products around religion, ingredients and local expectations, while dishes crossing borders can develop into famous new versions of their own.

This C1–C2 Fast Food & Globalization ESL lesson examines adaptation, authenticity, local identity and the tension between greater variety and the growing sameness of global consumer culture.

Students work with advanced expressions, decide what a restaurant should change abroad, defend a global-chain menu item against local criticism and write an extended argument addressing the strongest opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

You land somewhere you have never been. The signs are unreadable, the money looks strange, and you are tired and hungry. Then, across the street, the golden arches appear. For a moment the building feels like a home away from home. You walk in, and the menu is not quite the one you know.
