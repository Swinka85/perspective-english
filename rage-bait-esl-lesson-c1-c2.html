ABOUT THIS LESSON

About This Rage Bait ESL Lesson

Rage bait turns anger into engagement. Provocative posts can spread because every furious reply, mocking screenshot and argument signals attention to the algorithm.

This C1–C2 Rage Bait ESL lesson examines how outrage is monetized, how users become part of the distribution network, and where the chain from provocation to profit can realistically be broken.

Students work with advanced idiomatic language, evaluate who pays the cost of different interventions, defend a proposal for attracting readers without rage bait and write an extended argument addressing the strongest opposing view.

FROM THE ARTICLE

Someone posts that cereal should be eaten with orange juice, or that a much-loved film is worthless, or an opinion so absurd it hardly deserves a reply. Thousands of people reply anyway, furious.
